# Client Revision Governance

## Technical Reference & Operating Handbook: Content Production Agency Capacity & Sprint Velocity Planner
Comprehensive documentation and standard operating procedures for **Content Production Agency Capacity & Sprint Velocity Planner** within the **content** domain.

### Executive Overview
Develop a professional content agency capacity planning system: 1) Mathematical resource model calculating realistic monthly creative throughput per FTE (designer, video editor, copywriter) with built-in revision buffers, 2) Client revision governance framework (limiting free revision rounds), 3) Python sprint calculator (`calc_capacity.py`), 4) Markdown onboarding guide for account managers.

---

## Core Operational Principles

1. **Deterministic & Auditable Workflows:** Every parameter, threshold, and scoring rule must be grounded in observable telemetry rather than intuition.
2. **Regional Compliance & Localization:** Full adherence to GCC (Saudi GAMR, ZATCA, SAMA) and Egypt regulatory frameworks for e-commerce and digital communications.
3. **Fail-Safe Operational Guardrails:** Built-in safeguards against runaway ad spend, deliverability burn, or unmonitored lead SLA breaches.
